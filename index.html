Behind the Lens at LOBAH VISUALS

LOBAH VISUALS is a Lagos-based creative media brand built on one belief: every brand, event and milestone has a story worth telling well. We blend mobile-first videography with cinematic editing to create content that looks expensive without the expensive turnaround time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your project details. We'll discuss your needs, send a quotation, and schedule your session once confirmed.

How much do you charge?

Pricing depends on the type of project, duration and deliverables. We'll give you a clear, itemized quote on WhatsApp after a short discussion.

Do you travel?

Yes — we cover events and shoots across Lagos, with travel for out-of-state projects available on request.

How long does editing take?

Turnaround depends on project scope — short-form social content is typically faster than full event highlight reels or documentaries. We'll confirm a timeline before you book.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a deposit secures your date on the calendar. The remaining balance is due on or before delivery, as agreed during booking.

Can I reschedule?

Life happens — message us as early as possible on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to find a new date that works for both sides.

Can I book a consultation online?

Yes — paid consultations can be done over a call, so you don't need to be in Lagos to plan your shoot with us.
